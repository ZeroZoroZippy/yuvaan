## Brandintelle SVG Enhancements Explained

I updated two concept SVGs to make the visuals feel more product-real and easier to understand at a glance.

### 1) `brandintelle-bi-hub.svg`

What changed:
- Replaced the old placeholder "Signal map" circles and connecting line with a mini 6-bar chart.
- Added channel labels below the bars: `TV`, `Digital`, `Print`, `OOH`, `Radio`, `BTL`.
- Added missing text labels in the "Cross-module clarity" panel:
  - `Ads vs Budget`
  - `Spend vs Plan`
  - `Consumer Signal`

Why this change:
- The old node graphic looked abstract and did not clearly communicate real analytics behavior.
- A bar chart immediately signals "performance by channel," which is more believable for a BI dashboard concept.
- The unlabeled bars in "Cross-module clarity" looked unfinished. Labels now explain what each bar represents.

How it helps the portfolio presentation:
- Makes the concept look closer to a real product screen, not placeholder art.
- Improves storytelling for viewers scanning quickly (recruiters, clients, collaborators).
- Shows stronger information design and UX thinking in the case study.

What the visual result looks like:
- The top-left card now reads like a compact channel-performance widget.
- The top-right card now has clear, meaningful progress labels instead of empty bars.
- Overall tone, layout, and color palette remain consistent with the original design.

### 2) `brandintelle-ai-prototyping.svg`

What changed:
- Added labels to the 3 bars in the "AI layer" panel:
  - `Scope clarity`
  - `Prototype speed`
  - `Internal alignment`
- Added bold `AI` text inside the top-right ring decoration area (around `x=1195, y=258`).

Why this change:
- The three bars looked like placeholders with no meaning. Labels now make them communicative.
- The circular rings were visually interesting but ambiguous. Adding `AI` gives that element a clear role.

How it helps the portfolio presentation:
- Better communicates the value of AI in your workflow with concrete language.
- Reduces visual ambiguity and makes the composition feel intentional.
- Strengthens narrative clarity for social posts, case studies, and presentations.

What the visual result looks like:
- The "AI layer" block now reads as a focused capability panel with named outcomes.
- The ring motif now works as an "AI target/indicator" rather than generic decoration.
- The original style, canvas size, and dark warm palette are preserved.
